Linking Hiryu and the WMS

For Shaun. Ninja Kilat WMS runs the dark-store floor for GrabMart Kilat (Kahf and Labore at MA5, then KJ5): putaway, picking, packing, counting and restock. Hiryu stays the only link to Grab. From 30 September the plan is to connect the two by API. The WMS side of that link is written down in the PRD, §0.6; the points marked open there are for us to agree with you before either side builds.

Start with §0.6 The Hiryu link: six messages, the rules they share, what the WMS already has, and eight open items.

What we need from you

  1. Whether Hiryu can use Grab's Edit order to drop a missing line, or cancels with 2001.
  2. Hiryu's endpoints and payload for stock level, order ready and item short.
  3. How the catalogue (stores, menus, SKUs) reaches the WMS: on every change, on request, or both.
  4. That message 1 fires on Accept and retries until the WMS confirms.
  5. What Hiryu does when the WMS does not answer: pause the hub, or alert.
  6. The switch-on day: a test store on dev first, then one moment where Hiryu stops its own stock deduction.
  7. Who is alerted on each side when messages fail.
  8. Whether message 1 can carry Grab's estimated ready time.

Details for each are in §0.6.4.
